# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Jumat Berkah to be a welcoming project for people who want to improve transparent donation and community reporting software.

Project participants should be respectful, constructive, and patient.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Protect donor privacy and avoid sharing sensitive data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private donor, admin, payment, server, or credential data.
- Sharing exploit details publicly before maintainers can respond.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.
